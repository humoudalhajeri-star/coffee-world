The Problem

AI produces a lot… and you lose a lot.

Every day you ask ChatGPT or Claude dozens of questions. You get smart answers, working code, clever ideas — then you forget where you said them, and ask the same thing again a week later. The problem isn't the AI. It's its memory.

🌀

Endless conversations

Thousands of messages across dozens of threads. No index, no reliable search, no way back.

🗑️

Lost prompts

You crafted a perfect prompt, then closed the tab. Now you're fumbling with worse wordings for days instead of minutes.

🔁

Costly repetition

You re-ask the AI the same question 10 times because you forgot you asked it before. Time and money wasted.

📦

Scattered knowledge

Code in one chat, an idea in another, a note in a third. Nothing connected.

The Solution

One place that remembers for you.

AI Memory isn't just another notes app — it's a structured system built for AI output specifically, with six types, each with its own identity and purpose.

1

Save in one tap

Paste from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Open your memory, pick a type, save. Two seconds.

2

Organize smartly

Every entry has a type (prompt, code, note, answer, idea, template) + source + tags + an optional star.

3

Search instantly

⌘K from anywhere. Full-text search across titles, content and tags with instant highlighting.

4

Reuse cleverly

Prompts support {{variables}} — fill them in and copy the ready-to-paste result. A counter shows how often you've used each.

5

Share or sell

Copy, share via any app, or export as JSON / Markdown / PDF. Coming soon: sell collections with pricing you define.
